Industry Clarification

“Isn't it already in the cloud?” — what Microsoft protects, and what it doesn't

Microsoft protects the service
  • Uptime of the platform
  • Data centre infrastructure
  • Protection against hardware failure on their side
You protect your data (RiverIT covers this)
  • Accidental and malicious deletion
  • Ransomware that syncs into the cloud
  • Departed-employee data
  • Retention beyond Microsoft's default limits
  • Misconfigured or broken policies

Long-term retention · Offline copies

Some data you restore. Some data you keep.

Archiving is for the data your business must retain for years — finished projects, compliance records, historical files — kept safely offline and out of your production systems.

OPERATIONAL RECOVERY

Backup

A copy of your live data, taken frequently, kept so you can recover quickly from failure, deletion or attack. Optimised for speed of restore.

LONG-TERM RETENTION

Archive

The long-term home for data you no longer use daily but must keep. Optimised for retention, portability and cost — and best kept offline, where ransomware can't reach it.

Hardware Solution

Overland-Tandberg RDX QuikStor

A rugged, removable disk system that combines the speed of hard drives with the portability of tape cartridges. Load a cartridge, archive your data, and store it off-site or in a safe.

  • • Fast disk-based read/write — no tape drive wait times
  • • Rugged, portable cartridges built for transport and storage
  • • Simple drag-and-drop usability — no specialist software needed
  • • Ideal for offline, air-gapped copies
Honest Considerations
Worth knowing: per-terabyte, RDX cartridges cost more than tape. For most SMB archive volumes the speed and simplicity outweigh the difference — for very large archives, we'll tell you honestly if tape or cloud archiving fits better.
